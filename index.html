    Safety Guide

    Practical, calm guidance. Nirbhaya Net is a supplementary coordination tool — you can contact emergency services directly at any time.

    In immediate danger

    Call 112 directly. If you can, move to a populated, well-lit place and share your live location only with someone you trust.

    Prepare your circle

    Choose contacts who can respond quickly, confirm they consent to being contacted, and agree on what an SOS from you means.

    Know your route

    Share your expected route and arrival time with a trusted contact when travelling, especially at night or in unfamiliar areas.

    Trust your judgement

    If a situation feels unsafe, it is reasonable to leave, seek help, or ask for company. No tip guarantees safety — prioritise getting to support.

    After an incident

    Reach out to someone you trust, preserve relevant information if you choose to report, and consider professional support. Seeking help is a strength.

    Emergency numbers

    112 — India’s national emergency helpline. Nirbhaya Net does not replace 112; it adds a coordination layer for your trusted circle.
